Six-Week FYM Project

Scholarship Opportunity Finder

Build a centralized, student-friendly digital tool for discovering lesser-known scholarships, grants, and other funding opportunities.

Apply to Join Project

The Problem

Useful opportunities are hard to find.

Students looking for scholarships, grants, and funding often have to navigate scattered or spammy resources.

What We're Building

One clearer place to look.

The goal is a centralized digital tool or database that makes lesser-known opportunities easier for students to discover and understand.

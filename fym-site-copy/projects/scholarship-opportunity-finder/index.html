Current Project · Six-Week Sprint

Scholarship Opportunity Finder

A student-built project exploring a clearer way to discover and understand lesser-known scholarships, grants, and other funding opportunities.

Apply to Join Project

The Problem

Useful opportunities are spread across too many places.

Finding scholarships, grants, and other funding can be frustrating. Information is scattered across different websites, while some scholarship-search sites feel cluttered, repetitive, or spammy.

What We're Building

A more student-friendly way to look.

The team will explore a centralized digital tool or database that makes opportunities easier to discover and understand. This is not meant to be another giant list: students will help decide how information should be researched, organized, presented, and eventually accessed.

What Students Would Actually Do

Three areas, working together.

Research provides useful information. Design determines how students interact with it. Development turns the experience into something functional.

01

Tech / Development

Help turn the concept into a functioning digital experience. Work could include developing or improving the interface, organizing how opportunity information is handled, connecting parts of the experience, testing functionality, and fixing technical or usability problems.

02

Research & Data

Research scholarships, grants, and funding from reliable sources. Organize information consistently; check eligibility, deadlines when available, and official application links; and identify what students need when comparing opportunities.

03

UI / UX Design

Decide how opportunities should be displayed, organize information visually, design screens and layouts, improve mobile usability, and test whether the experience is clear and intuitive.

Project Goal

Work toward a useful first version.

By the end of the sprint, the team should be working toward a usable version that demonstrates how FYM can make scholarship and funding discovery simpler and more student-friendly.

Six-Week Execution Sprint

A framework for making something tangible.

The sprint keeps the team focused on one shared outcome rather than disconnected club assignments.

Week 1

Research & Understand

Study existing resources and determine what information students actually need.

Week 2

Plan

Define the first version, organize the information, and divide responsibilities.

Week 3

Build

Move research, design, and development from planning into execution.

Week 4

Test

Use the work as a student would and find confusing, missing, or broken pieces.

Week 5

Improve

Use feedback to strengthen the parts that matter most.

Week 6

Finish & Publish

Bring the work into a version that can be shared, demonstrated, or published.
